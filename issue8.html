In psychology of religion, we discuss commitment in terms of congregational and intrinsic/extrinsic motivation.     Abstract

    Certain economic concepts discussed in Summa Theologiae, are inherently analysed in the debate related to virtues and vices, in a theory of the individual‟s moral faith. Essentially, we note that, in Summa Theologiae, the economic issues, on which we stop briefly in the present study, were treated from the viewpoint of morality (virtue and justice).
