Past event 23–24 October 2025

ESRARC 2025 — 14th European Symposium on Religious Art Restoration & Conservation

Casa Universitarilor (a historic building dating to 1800), 11 Carol I Blvd., Iași, Romania

The 14th edition of ESRARC, held in memory of Dr. Iulian Rusu, brought together contributions across two tracks: the socio-spiritual and cultural significance of religious art (artistic and cultural evaluation; historical, theological and social perspectives; religion and cross-cultural communication; religion and globalization; religion, conflict and cultural heritage) and the conservation and restoration of religious heritage itself (environmental impact; analytical, diagnostic and intervention methods; new conservation and restoration materials; monitoring and scheduled maintenance).

Symposium chair: Nicoleta Melniciuc Puică, PhD, “Alexandru Ioan Cuza” University of Iași, Romania.

Previous editions: ESRARC 2023 · ESRARC 2022 · ESRARC 2019 · ESRARC 2018 · ESRARC 2017 · ESRARC 2016
